Privacy Policy - Sky Stack

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Sky Stack is a casual arcade game published by LLETMONK ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information We Collect

Sky Stack does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app requires no account, no sign-in, and no internet permissions for gameplay.

Local Data

Your high score is saved locally on your device only. It never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

Third-Party Services

The app currently contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. If this changes in a future update (for example, adding ads), this policy will be updated and the app's Play Store data-safety section revised accordingly.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and collects no data from any user, including children.
